The problem

You're moving fast.
The cracks are starting to show.

AI isn't just a productivity tool. It's a pressure test on how your organisation thinks, decides, and holds together. Most leadership teams are facing one or more of these right now:

Teams are moving faster but alignment is getting harder, not easier.
You have no shortage of AI ideas. The bottleneck is knowing which ones are worth pursuing.
Management got a lot more complex, but no one redesigned how decisions get made.
Your AI strategy is a series of pilots with no coherent position on where you're going.
You're accumulating tool dependencies faster than you can evaluate their long-term risk.
Your best people are questioning whether this is the right place to do meaningful work.

"In a landscape being rapidly flattened by AI, humanness is the moat and coherence is the bridge."

How we work

What happens when you reach out

We don't do proposals before conversations. Here's what the first few weeks actually look like.

01

30-minute conversation

We talk about what's actually going on. No pitch deck. If we can help, we'll tell you how. If we can't, we'll tell you that too and point you somewhere useful.

02

Diagnosis

We spend time understanding your specific situation before recommending anything. Organisational strain, AI adoption, and innovation overload are almost always connected, and the work needs to address them together. For many organisations this starts with a standalone AI Work Readiness Diagnostic: a 2-4 week assessment that maps where AI is stressing coherence and what needs to be redesigned.

03

A scoped engagement

We work on a defined problem with a defined outcome, not open-ended retainers. We bring in the right people from our network for what's needed, rather than a generalist team stretched across everything.

04

You own what we build

The goal is to leave your organisation more capable, not more dependent. We design for your people to carry the work forward without us.
